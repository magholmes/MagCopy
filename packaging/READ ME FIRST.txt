MagCopy
=======

1. Extract this folder somewhere (right-click the zip -> Extract All).
2. Double-click "Install MagCopy.bat".

That is the whole thing. It copies MagCopy into your own user folder, adds a Start menu
and desktop shortcut, and starts it. No administrator rights, nothing written outside
your account, no setup questions.

If you would rather not install anything, just double-click MagCopy.exe where it is.
It works exactly the same; you only lose the shortcuts.


Using it
--------

  Ctrl+Shift+A    Drag a rectangle over anything. The screenshot goes straight to your
                  clipboard - no preview, no save dialog. Paste it wherever you were going.

  Ctrl+Shift+S    Drag a rectangle and record it. Escape stops the recording and opens the
                  editor, where you can trim and crop before saving. The GIF comes out as
                  large and sharp as will fit under Discord's 10 MB limit.

Both shortcuts are only defaults. Click either one in the MagCopy window and press whatever
combination you want. MagCopy itself lives in the notification area, by the clock.


If Windows or Chrome complains
------------------------------

The build is not code-signed, so Windows has no reputation for it and may show
"Windows protected your PC" - choose More info, then Run anyway.

The source is at https://github.com/magholmes/MagCopy if you would rather read it and
build it yourself.


Removing it
-----------

Run "Uninstall MagCopy.bat" from the installed folder:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\MagCopy

Your saved screenshots and GIFs are never touched.
